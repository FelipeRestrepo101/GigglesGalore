An interview with
the leadership team

Interviewer: "So Giuliano (CEO) what inspires you the most in driving the company forward?"
Giuliano: "For me its never really been about the profits, cutting margins, KPI's, all that jazz, for me its really all about the laugh, because when you hear a good laugh you know you have a quality product, and you can just sort of feel the way the world lights up around it. Then the rest comes naturally."
Interviewer: "Guthrey (CIO/COO) ? . . ."
Guthrey: "I have to say the work here is not easy, and this all definitely wouldn't be possible without the incredible team we have here. To answer your question though, I completley agree with Giuliano, because when you've been staring at the screen for 6 hours and suddenly you feel that quality laugh go off next door, or over the phone, it really helps us all remember why we do what we do here."
